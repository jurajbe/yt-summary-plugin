How to use

  • Open any YouTube video. The panel sits at the top of the right-hand sidebar.
  • ⚡ The point: the payoff in 1–3 sentences and where it's revealed. 📋 Key points: bullet takeaways. 📖 Deep dive: section-by-section breakdown.
  • Click any timestamp to jump there. Ask follow-up questions in the box underneath.
  • Shortcut: Alt+Shift+S (change it at brave://extensions/shortcuts).
